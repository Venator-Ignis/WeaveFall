# Image Credits

Art direction: WeaveFall Art Bible and WeaveFall Art Prompts, Thread and Grisaille. Raster book illustrations and model sheets were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. Vector maps, compass and emblem were prepared from the campaign and its art bible; unspecified geography remains proposed cartography. Earlier map labels use cached Homebrewery Bookinsanity fonts embedded within their SVG masters. The functional SVG batch uses system Georgia / Times serif without embedded font or image data; its reviewed PNG exports preserve those labels. Emblems, calendars and encounter diagrams are source-based editable vector drawings, with proposed designs and staging recorded separately. Watercolour masks are mirrored unchanged from Homebrewery; their original asset URLs are recorded in the manifest. Mask, font and rendering provenance: [NaturalCrit's Homebrewery project](https://github.com/naturalcrit/homebrewery).

Selected files, hashes and roles are recorded in the asset manifest. Model names and seeds unavailable from the generation tool were not invented.
